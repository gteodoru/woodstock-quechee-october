Woodstock & Quechee, Oct 1–5

A calm, kid-paced foliage trip for Gabriel, his wife, their 22-month-old, and the au pair. Four nights, five calendar days. Car naps on the drives; lodging naps Friday–Sunday. Early dinners, bedtime at 8.

The plan at a glance

Overview

Why Woodstock

A soft yes as the base: village green, covered bridge, farm, and national park a short drive from Quechee. Enough for adults and an au pair without stacking tourist-trap days. Stay west of the village if you can — kitchen + living room is how Friday–Sunday naps actually happen.

The drives

He naps in the car both ways. Time departure so 12:30–2:30 is on the road after a midway play stop, and arrive as he wakes. Play stop ~1.5–2 hours from Old Greenwich (Elizabeth Park, or Wickham), then ~2.5–3 hours remaining. Route: I-95 → I-91 → US-4. Add a foliage-weekend buffer on I-91 and US-4. Do not use Montshire as the main stop — it is too late for the remaining-drive math.

Harvest weekend foliage

Oct 1–5 sits in the southern Vermont peak window — more color than mid-September, and more crowds. Saturday–Sunday is Billings Farm’s Harvest Celebration (Oct 3–4, not Oct 4–5). Book lodging first, reserve Worthy for Fri/Sat/Sun, and keep afternoons short. The Woodstock Apples & Crafts Fair is Oct 10–11 — a different weekend; skip it.

Daily rhythm

  1. Morning Out before ~12
  2. Nap 12:30–2:30 — car Thu & Mon; lodging Fri–Sun
  3. Afternoon Soft landing / back by ~6–6:30
  4. Dinner ~5:30–6:30
  5. Bed 8:00 p.m.

Where you sleep

Lodging · 4 nights

Check in Thursday, October 1. Check out Monday, October 5. Kitchen + living room is the whole strategy. Ground floor if you can get it. Confirm a crib before you put down a deposit.

Primary

On The River Inn · Farmhouse 2BR

1653 W Woodstock Rd, Woodstock · Oct 1–5

Separate farmhouse building: full kitchen, living/dining, two bedrooms, two baths. Prefer the ground-floor courtyard two-bedroom so you are not carrying a sleeping toddler up stairs. Hotel perks (indoor pool, s’mores) sit behind a nightly amenity fee — confirm the current amount when you book.

  • Ask for a crib / pack-and-play in writing
  • West of the village — quieter, easy Friday–Sunday nap return
  • 506 Bistro is on site if you need a backup meal

The kitchen-night backbone: groceries, prepared food, coffee. Flagship on W. Woodstock Road, handy to On The River. Closed Mondays — shop Thu–Sun.
